Client: Rimal Boutique (rimalboutique.com)

What product/service is being promoted?
  Rimal Boutique is an online women's fashion boutique based in Canada.
  It offers limited-edition dresses, two-piece sets, kimonos and
  couture pieces inspired by Gulf (GCC) elegance with a modern
  feminine touch, plus jewelry, makeup and perfumes.

Who is the target audience?
  Women aged 20-45 in Canada and the USA, especially Arab and Muslim
  women and modest-fashion lovers who want elegant, unique pieces for
  weddings, Eid, parties and special occasions, without travelling
  to the Gulf to shop.

Which platforms are most suitable?
  Instagram and TikTok - fashion is visual, so Reels, try-on videos
  and outfit styling reach our audience best.
  Snapchat - very popular with Arab and Gulf audiences.
  Pinterest - women search there for occasion and modest outfit ideas.

Proposed Hashtags:
  #RimalBoutique #GulfElegance #ModestFashionCanada
  #EidOutfits #ArabFashionCanada

Promotional Strategy (2-3 sentences):
  Rimal Boutique will post short try-on Reels and TikToks that show how
  each limited piece moves and fits, along with behind-the-scenes
  videos of new arrivals. Before Eid and wedding season, we will run
  "limited drop" countdowns and partner with Arab-Canadian fashion
  influencers to build trust and urgency. Every post will link to
  rimalboutique.com and highlight free shipping on orders over $350.

Note:
  Rimal Boutique is a real small business owned by the student.
  This plan was created for the CA-FWBDV academic project.
  The project web page is a separate demo page and does not
  change the live store website.
